Privacy Policy

Vinny Social Manager · Last updated: 9 November 2026

Data controller

Vincenzo Stante, Bari, Italy – personal project Vinnynow. Contact: v.stante@gmail.com

What data we process

Only data of the creator's own TikTok account, obtained through TikTok Login Kit with the creator's consent: account identifier (open_id), display name and avatar, access and refresh tokens, the videos the creator chooses to publish, and the comments on the creator's own posts.

Why

Solely to publish the creator's videos to his own account and to read and reply to comments on his own posts. No profiling, no advertising, no sale or transfer of data to third parties.

Storage and retention

Tokens are stored encrypted as secrets of a private repository and used only by the tool. They are deleted when access is revoked (TikTok settings → Security → Manage app permissions) or on request. Comments are processed only to prepare replies and are not stored beyond what is needed for that purpose.

Your rights

Under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), articles 15–22, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability or object to processing by writing to v.stante@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).
