Vinny Social Manager

Vinnynow · around the world and beyond

Vinny Social Manager is a personal tool used by Vincenzo Stante, creator of the Vinnynow travel channel, to publish his own travel videos to his own TikTok account and to read and reply to comments on his own posts. Every publication is reviewed and approved by the creator before it goes live. The tool is not offered to third parties.
